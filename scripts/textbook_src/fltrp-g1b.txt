# 外研版（一年级起点）一年级下册

## Unit 1 Feelings | 第一单元 感受

### Let's chant! | 一起说唱
words: happy, today, hand
I'm happy, I'm happy, I'm happy today. | 我很开心，我很开心，我今天很开心。
Clap, clap, clap my hands. | 拍拍拍，拍拍手。
One, two, three! | 一，二，三！

### Don't do that again | 别再这样了
words: follow, walk, away, hungry, tired, sad, scared, happy, sorry, again
Dear all, please follow me. | 大家请跟着我。
Don't walk away. | 不要走开。
Oh no. Mum? Mum? | 哎呀。妈妈？妈妈？
Jack is hungry. He is tired, too. | 杰克饿了。他也累了。
Jack is sad. He is scared, too. | 杰克很难过。他也很害怕。
Jack, you are here! | 杰克，你在这儿！
Oh, Mum. I'm so happy. | 哦，妈妈。我太开心了。
I'm happy, too. | 我也很开心。
Don't do that again. | 别再这样了。
Sorry, Mum. | 对不起，妈妈。

## Unit 2 Family | 第二单元 家人

### Let's chant! | 一起说唱
words: dad, mum, brother, sister, grandpa, grandma, together, love
Dad, mum, brother, sister. | 爸爸，妈妈，哥哥，姐姐。
Grandpa, grandma, all together. | 爷爷，奶奶，全家在一起。
Love, love, we love each other! | 爱，爱，我们彼此相爱！

### Happy birthday | 生日快乐
words: birthday, grandpa, grandma, mum, dad, brother, baby, sister, love, family
Hello! I'm Taotao. I'm 7. | 你好！我是涛涛。我七岁了。
This is for you, Taotao. | 这是给你的，涛涛。
Thank you! | 谢谢！
This is for Grandpa. | 这是给爷爷的。
This is for Grandma. | 这是给奶奶的。
This is for Mum. | 这是给妈妈的。
This is for Dad. | 这是给爸爸的。
This is for my brother. | 这是给我哥哥的。
This is for my baby sister. | 这是给我小妹妹的。
This is for me. | 这是给我自己的。
I love my family. | 我爱我的家人。

## Unit 4 At the farm | 第四单元 在农场

### Let's chant! | 一起说唱
words: seven, eight, nine, ten, eleven, twelve, count, again
One, two, three, four. | 一，二，三，四。
Five, six, seven, eight. | 五，六，七，八。
Nine, ten, eleven, twelve. | 九，十，十一，十二。
Let's count again. | 我们再数一遍。

### A happy morning at the farm | 农场里快乐的早晨
words: grandpa, grandma, great, morning, breakfast, work, farm, rabbit, cow, egg, home
Welcome! | 欢迎！
Grandpa! Grandma! Great to see you! | 爷爷！奶奶！见到你们真好！
It's 7 o'clock. Time to get up, children! | 七点了。孩子们，该起床了！
It's 8 o'clock. Time to have breakfast. | 八点了。该吃早饭了。
Yummy yummy. | 真好吃。
It's 9 o'clock. Let's work at the farm. | 九点了。我们去农场干活吧。
How many rabbits? | 有多少只兔子？
How many cows? | 有多少头奶牛？
How many eggs do you have? | 你有多少个鸡蛋？
I have 8. | 我有八个。
I have 7. | 我有七个。
It's 12 o'clock. Let's get back home. | 十二点了。我们回家吧。

## Unit 5 My room | 第五单元 我的房间

### Let's chant! | 一起说唱
words: bear, living, room, table, under
Teddy Bear, Teddy Bear, where are you? | 泰迪熊，泰迪熊，你在哪里？
Are you in the living room? Yes. Yes. Yes. | 你在客厅里吗？是的。是的。是的。
Are you on the table? No. No. No. | 你在桌子上吗？不是。不是。不是。
Are you under the sofa? No. No. No. | 你在沙发下面吗？不是。不是。不是。

### Where is it? | 它在哪里？
words: need, water, bottle, room, desk, schoolbag, behind, door, bed, under
Dad, I need my water bottle. | 爸爸，我要我的水壶。
OK. Where is it? | 好的。它在哪儿？
It's in my room. | 在我房间里。
Is it on my desk? | 在我的书桌上吗？
No, it's not there. | 不，不在那儿。
Is it in my schoolbag? | 在我的书包里吗？
Where is your schoolbag? | 你的书包在哪儿？
It's behind the door. | 在门后面。
No, your water bottle is not there. | 不，你的水壶不在那儿。
Is it on my bed? | 在我的床上吗？
No. | 不在。
Oh, it's under your bed. | 哦，在你的床底下。
