# 外研版（一年级起点）二年级上册

## Unit 1 Face | 第一单元 脸

### Let's chant! | 一起说唱
words: face, eye, mouth, nose, snowman, love
Eyes on the face. | 脸上有眼睛。
A mouth on the face. | 脸上有嘴巴。
A nose on the face. | 脸上有鼻子。
This is a snowman. | 这是一个雪人。
We love the snowman. | 我们喜欢这个雪人。

### Story | 故事
words: twins, big, small, eye, nose, mouth, long, short, hair, different, people, wrong, idea
Hi. I'm Meimei. | 你好，我是美美。
I'm Beibei. | 我是贝贝。
We are twins. | 我们是双胞胎。
I have big eyes. | 我有一双大眼睛。
I have big eyes, too. | 我也有一双大眼睛。
I have a small nose. | 我有一个小鼻子。
I have a small nose, too. | 我也有一个小鼻子。
I have a small mouth. | 我有一张小嘴巴。
I have a small mouth, too. | 我也有一张小嘴巴。
People often get us wrong. | 大家常常把我们认错。
What can we do? | 我们能怎么办呢？
I have an idea! | 我有个主意！
I have long hair. | 我留长头发。
I have short hair. | 我留短头发。
We are different now. | 现在我们不一样了。

## Unit 3 Animals | 第三单元 动物

### Let's chant! | 一起说唱
words: zoo, tiger, fox, bird, monkey
Let's go to the zoo. | 我们去动物园吧。
Look! | 看！
Tigers and foxes. | 老虎和狐狸。
Birds and monkeys. | 小鸟和猴子。

### Fox and Tiger | 狐狸和老虎
words: hungry, fox, tiger, eat, king, animal, show, rabbit, deer, run, fly
Tiger is hungry. | 老虎饿了。
A fox! Great! | 一只狐狸！太好了！
Don't eat me. I'm the king of animals! | 别吃我。我是百兽之王！
No. I'm the king of animals! | 不，我才是百兽之王！
Let me show you. Come with me. | 我证明给你看。跟我来。
Look! The rabbits run away. | 看！兔子们跑掉了。
The deer runs away, too. | 鹿也跑掉了。
The birds fly away. | 鸟儿们飞走了。
The monkeys run away, too. | 猴子们也跑掉了。
See? I'm the king of animals! | 看到了吧？我是百兽之王！
Yes, you are! | 是的，你是！

## Unit 4 Food | 第四单元 食物

### Let's chant! | 一起说唱
words: apple, banana, milk, like
Apples, apples. I like apples. | 苹果，苹果。我喜欢苹果。
Bananas, bananas. I like bananas. | 香蕉，香蕉。我喜欢香蕉。
Milk, milk. I like milk. | 牛奶，牛奶。我喜欢牛奶。
How about you? | 你呢？

### Picnic day | 野餐日
words: picnic, apple, banana, bread, milk, meat, share, food
It's picnic day! | 今天是野餐日！
I like apples and bananas. | 我喜欢苹果和香蕉。
I like bread and milk. | 我喜欢面包和牛奶。
I like meat. | 我喜欢肉。
We can share our food. | 我们可以分享我们的食物。
We all like our picnic. | 我们都喜欢我们的野餐。

## Unit 5 Festival | 第五单元 节日

### Let's chant! | 一起说唱
words: happy, festival, welcome, home, wish
Happy Spring Festival! | 春节快乐！
Welcome to my home. | 欢迎来我家。
Let's have a good time. | 我们一起玩得开心吧。
Best wishes to you! | 送给你最美好的祝福！
Happy Spring Festival! | 春节快乐！

### A happy festival | 快乐的节日
words: welcome, celebrate, festival, wear, red, clothes, luck, door, lantern, beautiful, window, candy, interesting
Welcome, Joe. | 欢迎你，乔。
Hi, Lanlan. How do you celebrate the Spring Festival? | 你好，兰兰。你们怎么过春节？
We wear red clothes for the festival. | 过节时我们穿红衣服。
Look here. What is this? | 看这里。这是什么？
It's fu. It means good luck. We put it on the door. | 这是“福”，意思是好运。我们把它贴在门上。
Look at these red lanterns. | 看这些红灯笼。
They are beautiful! | 它们真漂亮！
We also put red paper-cuts on the windows. | 我们还在窗户上贴红色的剪纸。
Wow. They are great! | 哇，它们真棒！
Joe, come and have some jiaozi. | 乔，来吃点饺子吧。
Oh. What is this? A red candy. | 哦，这是什么？一颗红色的糖。
It's lucky jiaozi. It means good luck for the new year! | 这是幸运饺子，代表新的一年好运连连！
I like the Spring Festival. It's so interesting. | 我喜欢春节，它太有意思了。
